# Artwork direction

Status: proposed creative brief; no artwork generated yet.

MENACE Genesis should feel like a premium cinematic 3D character collection: an unmistakable central figure, believable sculptural form, intentional asymmetry, rich tactile surfaces and controlled contrast. Use a consistent square composition, camera height and lighting language while allowing silhouettes to distinguish all nine types. Aim for a high-resolution master (proposed 4096 × 4096) and verify Puffles and the eventual XRPL platform's supported export sizes and formats before locking delivery. Keep the face readable at thumbnail size and place fine details where zooming reveals them.

## Background rule

Exactly one visual influence per NFT: choose **one** of monolithic architecture, industrial machinery, desert landscape, deep ocean, celestial space, or urban night. Each is a proposed category, not a reference to a specific franchise or artwork. Use one clean environment, a limited palette, subdued depth, and clear negative space around the figure. Do not combine or collage influences, landmarks, logos, franchise characters or multiple settings. Lighting and lore details support the scene but do not introduce a second influence. Record the selected influence in metadata and check it visually.

## Production

1. Establish a style frame and one master character for owner approval; check silhouette, material realism, thumbnail readability and zoomable detail.
2. Produce modular, consistently registered transparent layers only where compositing preserves realistic lighting, shadows and depth. Otherwise use individually rendered finished images or 1/1 imports where the chosen tool supports them.
3. Name and organize trait layers under `artwork/layers/<trait-type>/<trait-value>.png`; keep source renders and rights records separately. Place rare finished masters under `artwork/one-of-ones/`.
4. Use one verified lore motif per selected image; preserve a private production map of placement and source. Avoid placing an address or invented hash in art as if it were a verified transaction.
5. Review full-size and thumbnail images for anatomy, clipping, inconsistent shadows, legibility, duplicate images, unwanted marks and one-influence compliance.

Puffles does not create the artwork. Its documented layer export workflow must be tested with these assets; high-end 3D lighting may require finished renders instead of naive layering.
